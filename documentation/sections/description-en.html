Waste Management: Description back to ToC

Ontology that describes the elements associated with waste management, including facilities, containers and collection points.

Figure 1 shows the conceptual model of the ontology with its classes and relationships (object properties).

One of the core concepts of the ontology is the waste management facility. This facility is a type of waste facility that accepts and manages specific types of waste, which are represented through a taxonomy of waste type concepts. The classes categorized as waste facilities include the collection point and the waste container. Collection points can be either "fixed" or "mobile."

Waste management facilities feature a treatment process. They also have a management scope, which refers to the classification and storage of special types of waste. Waste facilities belong to a specific entity, such as a city district. Their postal address and geographic coordinates can be specified by reusing the GeoSPARQL vocabulary.
